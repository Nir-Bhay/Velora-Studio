## Deviations from the brief (disclosed)

1. **Time-stretch.** The narration, trimmed of silence, runs 71.2 s at normal speed. The film is 60 s, so
   the clips are time-stretched with `atempo=1.25`. Pitch is kept, and the stretched narration is about 57 s
   (about 140 words per minute). This is audible as a faster delivery. The 133-word VO is verbatim from the brief.
2. **Scene windows.** The brief's fixed windows were replaced with windows derived from the narration. Each scene
   gets its narration plus an equal share of the leftover gap (0.37 s). Actual windows:

   | # | Scene | Brief | Actual |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | hook | 00:00–05 | 0.00–4.63 |
   | 2 | announcement | 05–10 | 4.63–11.16 |
   | 3 | signature | 10–22 | 11.16–22.79 |
   | 4 | 1,600× estimate | 22–30 | 22.79–30.55 |
   | 5 | fan factories | 30–42 | 30.55–41.81 |
   | 6 | computing | 42–50 | 41.81–49.67 |
   | 7 | reality check | 50–56 | 49.67–56.62 |
   | 8 | closing | 56–60 | 56.62–60.00 |

3. **Voice.** The narration uses the English narration voice selected in the audition (voice-05). The selection
   trace does not show explicit user confirmation, so treat the choice as provisional.
4. **Captions.** Word timings are estimated from character weight and punctuation within each clip. They are
   not forced-aligned to the audio. Expect small timing offsets (under about 0.2 s).

## Accuracy checks

- Every number is traced to `research-notes.md`. The 1,600× and 30× are labelled theoretical. The 3.5× carries the required hardware caveat.
- The film does not say the internet, banks or TLS were broken. The one line with "internet" is the verbatim VO sentence "This isn't a mass internet breach."
- RSA-2048+ is stated as not threatened, per both papers.

## Audio

- Music and SFX are generated by code (`render/music.mjs`): pads, a kick pulse, hats, a riser, an impact after a
  short music drop before the 1,600× reveal, and chimes. No samples or recordings were used.
- The narration is ducked under the music with sidechain compression, then the mix is normalised to −14 LUFS.

## Visual design

Revision 2 replaced the earlier dark, slow-fade design after review. The brief's palette was kept in hue, but
lifted in lightness so the film reads bright on a phone.

- **Style:** kinetic typography and motion graphics, not paper-craft or slow fades. Each scene has a hook
  built from its own motion: a versus slam (scene 1), a fighter-card entrance (scene 2), a word-by-word
  equation and a block-fill warning (scene 3), a count-up to 1,600× with a shockwave (scene 4), a fan burst
  (scene 5), racing bars (scene 6), a split versus with a diagonal divider (scene 7), and a closing word
  slam (scene 8).
- **Research basis:** trends for vertical short-form motion design in 2026 (bold scale contrast,
  beat-timed type, marker highlights, glitch frames, split screens, pop-up text, and a strong first second).
- **Fight theme:** scene 1 is a literal versus ("AI VS RSA-1024"), and scene 7 is a clash across a diagonal divider.
- **On-screen text** follows the brief's wording for each scene. Scenes 5 and 6 previously used different
  wording and have been corrected to the brief.
- **No new claims:** every number and phrase is in `research-notes.md`. Required caveats are on screen.
- All visuals are drawn in code. No stock imagery, no Matrix code, no hooded figures, no fake CLI.
- Fonts are Inter and JetBrains Mono, both under the SIL Open Font License, from `@expo-google-fonts`.
- **Film grain** at low strength, and a slight breathing motion on the lock.

## QA performed

- ffmpeg stream check: 1800 frames, 60.00 s, correct codec, size, frame rate, pixel format and audio format.
- Loudness check: −14.0 LUFS, −1.5 dBTP.
- Frame review: stills across all eight scenes and at scene transitions, including the 1,600× reveal, the
  crossfades, and the hardware caveat, on revision 2. Review found and fixed these: the scene 3 block bar overlapping the
  equation, fan rays crowding "≈30×", the scene 6 headline overlapping the bars, the scene 6 grid overlapping its
  label, the scene 4 curve crossing its legend, and a crossfade bug where scene 7 had no alpha setup, so it hid
  scene 6 instead of blending with it.

## Limits

- I did not watch the full video in motion. QA was by sampled frames and stream analysis. A full playback pass on a phone is still recommended.
- The captions are estimated, not aligned (see deviation 4).
- Secondary sources only for DeepMind's announcement text. No independent cryptographer assessment.
- Music is synthesised, not composed. It is original, but simple.
